## Full Description

You ask ChatGPT or Claude to help you plan something. You get a great list. You copy it into Notion, or a note, or nowhere at all — and the moment the plan changes in the next conversation, you start over.

Living Checklist fixes that. It attaches a persistent, interactive checklist directly to each conversation. Your progress is saved in your browser, survives plan revisions, and is always one click away in the side panel.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS AS YOU WORK

Every checklist shows a live progress bar — X out of Y steps done — so you always know where you stand. Check off items as you complete them. When the last item is checked, the extension celebrates and offers to archive the finished plan or start a new one. Your state is stored locally in your browser and never sent anywhere.

MERGE, DON'T START OVER

This is the feature that makes Living Checklist different. When you ask ChatGPT or Claude to revise your plan, just click Merge latest. The extension compares the new version to your existing checklist, matches items by meaning rather than position, keeps your checked items checked, archives anything removed, and adds anything new. You never lose work just because the plan improved.

EXPORT AND SHARE YOUR PLANS

Copy any checklist to your clipboard as clean Markdown with one click — ready to paste into Notion, Linear, a GitHub issue, or anywhere else. You can also generate a shareable URL that encodes the entire plan. Anyone with the link and the extension can import it instantly. If your plan is too large for a URL, the extension tells you and suggests Export instead.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open a saved ChatGPT or Claude conversation in Chrome.
2. Click the Living Checklist icon in your toolbar to open the side panel.
3. Click "Capture from latest reply" — the extension reads the latest assistant message, extracts the list, and saves it to your browser.

After that, check off items as you work. When the plan changes, click Merge latest. Your progress is preserved automatically.

WORKS WITH CHATGPT AND CLAUDE

Living Checklist supports both ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Claude (claude.ai). The side panel header shows which AI assistant you are connected to. All parsing and merge logic works the same way on both platforms.

A LIBRARY FOR ALL YOUR PLANS

The Library tab shows every checklist you have saved, sorted by most recently updated. Search by title or task text to find what you need. Open any checklist to continue working on it, or click through to the original conversation.

YOUR DATA STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER

No account required. No servers. No data leaves your device. Everything is stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your machine. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.

Living Checklist is for builders, consultants, and anyone who uses AI to plan things and actually wants to execute them. If you have ever lost your place in a plan because the conversation moved on, this extension is for you.
