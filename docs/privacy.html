Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Living Checklist for ChatGPT is a Chrome extension that captures AI-generated plans from ChatGPT and Claude into a persistent, interactive checklist in the browser side panel.

Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the extension's features:

  • Checklist data — Items captured from your ChatGPT and Claude conversations are stored locally in Chrome's built-in storage (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable cloud sync (see below).
  • Email address (optional) — If you sign in to enable cross-device sync, we collect your email address solely to send a one-time login code (OTP). Your email is used for authentication only and is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Cloud Sync (Optional)

Cloud sync is disabled by default. If you choose to sign in, your checklists are stored securely in our database (Supabase). You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not read, store, or transmit the content of your ChatGPT or Claude conversations.
  • We do not collect browsing history, usage analytics, or any personally identifiable information beyond what is listed above.
  • We do not use cookies or third-party tracking.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — used for optional cloud sync and authentication. Supabase Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Local checklist data is stored in your browser until you uninstall the extension or clear the data manually. Cloud-synced data is retained until you delete your account.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
